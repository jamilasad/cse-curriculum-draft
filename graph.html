Prerequisite graph

Every course package (a theory course with its lab) is a box, placed in its semester. An arrow runs from a course to a course that needs it. Each box links to its course page. Hover over or focus a box, or pick a course below, to show everything it needs and everything that needs it.

  • prerequisite or gate
  • co-requisite (same semester)
  • "Track Course I" of any track, into Capstone I

If a student fails a course, what moves?

Pick a track, the course failed and how many times. The simulator moves every course that needs it, directly or through other courses, and shows the new semester of each and the effect on graduation. It assumes every course runs every semester (two intakes a year) and that the retake is passed in the next semester. It does not check timetable clashes; it warns when a semester goes over 24 contact hours or five theory courses.

The result appears here. It needs JavaScript.

Examples from the specification (3.5): failing CSE-2425 Computer Organization & Architecture delays CSE-3541 Operating Systems; failing CSE-2440 Core Competency Assessment until the end of semester 6 delays the semester-7 track courses and CSE-4700 Capstone Project I. Note that CSE-2440 has two sittings in its own semester and can be retaken in semesters 5 and 6 without delay.
