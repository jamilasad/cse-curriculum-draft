Transition and equivalence

Not yet drafted. Draft 1 of the curriculum does not cover the move from the 2022 syllabus. This page lists the questions it must answer. Nothing below is decided.

Questions this page must answer

From the website specification, section 3.7:

  • Which batches follow which curriculum, and the first-offering calendar for new courses.
  • An equivalence table for retakes: what a student who failed a 2022 course takes once that course stops running.
  • Rules for irregular and transfer students.

What already exists to start from

The per-course table of 2022 fates shows, for every 2022 course, the new course or courses that take its place. It is a starting point for the equivalence table, not an equivalence ruling.
