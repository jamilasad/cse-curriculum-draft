Capstone

Framework section 11. Capstone (CSE-4700 and CSE-4800)

Teams of three or four work for two semesters on a problem in their track with a real client, user group or dataset. Capstone I (3 credits) ends with an approved proposal, a design and a working prototype. Capstone II (5 credits) ends with a deployed or demonstrable system, a report and an individual defence. A research thesis is allowed in place of a product for students heading to graduate study.

Course pages: CSE-4700 Capstone Project I and CSE-4800 Capstone Project II. The design shared by both is in the shared design section.
