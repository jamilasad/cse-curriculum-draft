Industry Practice

Framework section 10. Industry Practice (CSE-4810, semester 8)

Vacations are too short for an internship, the intake is too large for every student to be placed on site, and the campus is far from employers. The course therefore requires at least 200 logged hours of supervised professional work in any of four modes:

ModeDescription
AOn-site or hybrid internship with a company
BRemote internship, or a verified part-time remote job
CVirtual studio: an on-campus team working a real backlog under an industry mentor
DOpen-source contribution sprint, assessed by accepted contributions

Semester 8 is built around it: only 11 scheduled hours per week, timetabled on two days, with the two theory courses suited to blended delivery. Students either do a six-week block at the start of the semester while classes run online, or work three days a week throughout. Semester-8 courses are planned accordingly: weeks 1–6 use only work that can be done remotely, and every supervised test, Level 0 quiz and viva falls in weeks 7–14 on campus. Assessment of Industry Practice is by logbook, supervisor's evaluation, a reflective report and a viva.

Full specification: CSE-4810 Industry Practice. Its sections: Rationale and what changed, Course learning outcomes, Minimum competency, Stretch goals, The four modes, Timetables, Calendar, Partner pipeline the department must build, Scaling to the intake, A student with no placement, Logbook, Supervisor's evaluation form, Reflective report, Viva, Assessment, Resources, Notes for the coordinator.
