AI-use levels

Framework section 6. AI in the curriculum

AI is treated three ways: as a tool whose use is staged, as a subject everyone studies, and as a specialisation.

Staged use. Every outline states the permitted level for each assessed component.

LevelMeaningWhere
0No AI assistanceProgramming I; all quizzes, written exams, supervised practical tests and vivas in every course
1AI as tutor: may explain concepts and errors, may not write the solutionSemester 1 assignments outside Programming I; weekly lab work and assignments in semesters 2–5; other lab projects in semesters 2–3
2AI as pair programmer, with disclosure: usage is logged, output is tested, the student defends every lineThe Object-Oriented Programming & Design team project (semester 3); lab projects and studios from semester 4; weekly lab work and assignments in semesters 6–8
3AI is the material: building, evaluating and operating AI systemsAI studio, AI & Data track, parts of other tracks

A course may set a stricter level than this table for named weeks, for example the opening "by hand" weeks of a track course; it may not set a looser one.

For everyone. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (S5, 3+1) gives the foundations. AI Application & Agent Engineering Studio (S6, 3 credits) has every student ship a deployed product with an AI feature, built with an AI-assisted workflow: LLM APIs, retrieval, tool use, agents, evaluation, cost and safety.

For specialists. The AI & Data track adds four courses. It needs stronger mathematics; the core AI courses do not.

Check against the framework

The script checks that every examination, quiz, practical test, viva and defence is at Level 0, and that assignments, lab work and projects do not exceed the level framework section 6 sets for the semester (Level 3 is allowed only in the AI studio, the tracks and the capstone). Reviewers should still read the wording.

Every component is within the levels of framework section 6, as far as this site can tell from the wording.
