Assessment schemes

Framework section 7. Assessment rules

Theory courses. The university rule stands: written mid-term and final examinations carry 80 marks, continuous assessment 20. What changes is the questions:

  • At least half of the written marks test application and analysis: reading and tracing code, finding and fixing defects, choosing between designs, working a case.
  • At most a fifth of the written marks test recall.
  • Continuous assessment (20): short in-class quizzes at Level 0 (10) and assignments or a mini-project (10).

Paired labs (100 marks).

ComponentMarks
Weekly lab work and lab record30
Two supervised practical tests, on lab machines or at the bench for hardware labs, Level 030
Lab project25
Individual viva on the student's own work15

A student who cannot explain their own submitted work in the viva does not pass the lab, whatever the project mark.

Studios (100 marks). Team product against milestones 40; individual contribution evidenced by commits and reviews 20; individual viva 20; documentation and demonstration 20.
