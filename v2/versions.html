v1 and v2 compared

v1 is Draft 1, the current draft. v2 is Flow B, an alternative course flow for discussion (24 November 2026). Flow B has not been adopted. Both versions are built from the same course outlines; v2 uses Flow B's own course data and adds the changes below.

v1 overview · v2 overview · Read the full Flow B proposal

What Flow B changes

The department's earlier competitive-programming plan has a sound goal: graduates who can solve unfamiliar coding problems quickly and correctly, because that is what Bangladeshi employers test. Flow B keeps that goal and most of the practice volume. It rejects the two parts that no longer work: Codeforces rating targets, and counts of solved problems used as evidence. Since 2025, AI assistants solve contest problems at world-final level, so neither ratings nor counts show what a student can do alone. In their place, Flow B awards levels only in supervised contests on the department's own judge. On entrepreneurship, Flow B gives every student 6 credits and offers an opt-in venture route that can use up to 11 more (capstone and Industry Practice). The design aims at businesses that Bangladeshi graduates can actually start and run.

Co-curricular, no credit

  • Problem-solving club. Contest training for the strong students (ICPC regionals, inter-university contests, Codeforces under its AI rule), with the coach's time counted in teaching load.
  • Monthly open supervised contest. For P-level certification, open to all semesters.
  • Interview season (S7–S8). The curated list of 50 interview problems, two supervised mock contests, and online mock interviews with alumni.
  • Venture club. Monthly founder talks online; demo day joined to the capstone showcase.
