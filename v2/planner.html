Study-plan builder

Pick a track and, if you wish, one substitution. The builder lays out the eight semesters and checks the plan against the prerequisites, the competency gate (CSE-2440 before the semester-7 track courses and Capstone I), the limits of 24 contact hours and five theory courses a semester, and the substitution rules of framework section 9. It is a planning aid for advisers, not a registration system.

The plan appears here. It needs JavaScript. Without it, use the roadmap and the substitution table on each track page.

Generated on 24 November 2026 from the curriculum files (framework, semester plan, course outlines and data). Edit those files and run the build again; do not edit these pages by hand.
