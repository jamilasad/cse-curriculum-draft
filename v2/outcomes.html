The current criteria set no numeric credit quotas by subject area; they require a minimum of 130 total credits and adequate coverage, judged qualitatively.

Programme outcomes PO1–PO12 are mapped course by course in the outlines. PO6–PO12 (sustainability, ethics, teamwork, communication, management, lifelong learning, entrepreneurship) are assessed mainly in the studios, CSE-4753, CSE-4805, CSE-4760, the capstone and Industry Practice.

Point to raise with the accreditation coordinator. With Calculus II added, mathematics totals 15 credits and natural science 4. Chemistry is gone, and natural science is the thinnest area; if the coordinator judges it too thin, the cheapest addition is a second 1-credit physics laboratory.
