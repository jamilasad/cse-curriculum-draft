The semester-8 rule

From framework section 10. Industry Practice

Semester 8 is built around it: only 11 scheduled hours per week, timetabled on two days, with the two theory courses suited to blended delivery. Students either do a six-week block at the start of the semester while classes run online, or work three days a week throughout. Semester-8 courses are planned accordingly: weeks 1–6 use only work that can be done remotely, and every supervised test, Level 0 quiz and viva falls in weeks 7–14 on campus.

Semester 8 in the data: 13 scheduled contact hours a week, 18 credits.
