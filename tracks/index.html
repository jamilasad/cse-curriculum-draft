Rules from framework section 9

Each student takes one track of four courses (3-credit theory with 1-credit lab each): one in semester 6, two in semester 7 and one in semester 8. The capstone is done in the track's area. Tracks are chosen at the end of semester 5.

Courses are listed in 01-semester-plan.md.

Overlap. Track Course I (semester 6) is each track's foundation and cannot be replaced. A student may replace one of Track Courses II–IV with a course from another track offered in the same semester, with the adviser's approval. A course that is a prerequisite of the student's own semester-8 track course cannot be swapped; in practice an AID student may swap only CSE-4783 and an INF student only CSE-4777. Natural substitutions, all in semester 7: Data Engineering & Analytics or Web & Application Security for SWE students; Cloud Infrastructure & Virtualization for SWE or SEC students; IoT Systems & Edge AI for AID students.

Running the tracks. With five to eleven sections per intake and separate male and female sections, not every track will fill in every section group. A track runs when at least 20 students choose it; smaller groups are merged across sections, with theory delivered in a shared or blended session where needed. EMB is capped by lab equipment.
