What happened to the 2022 courses

Every course of the Autumn 2022 syllabus (89 codes, 163 credits), with its fate in this draft, the new course or courses that take its place, the credits before and after, and the reason.

How each fate was found
  1. Framework section 14 groups old and new courses in rows; a 2022 course named in a row takes that row's new courses. The fate follows from the row: "Removed" with no new course is Dropped, "Removed" with new courses is Split or spread, "Merged into" is Merged; one old to one new is Kept, revised (same words) or Renamed; one old to several new is Split or spread; several old to one new is Merged; several to several is Replaced.
  2. Courses not in section 14 are matched by the same code (framework section 17 says codes are not reused for a different course), then by title.
  3. A lab named nowhere follows its theory course; this is marked as inferred.
  4. Where the examples in the website specification (3.6) name a course, their fate is used.
  5. Anything left is shown as Not placed.

"Credits after" is the credit of the new course or courses as a whole (theory with lab); one new course often takes the place of several old ones, so the column does not add up.
