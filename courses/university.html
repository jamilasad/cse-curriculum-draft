University courses (GED and MDP)

These courses are set by the university and kept as in the 2022 syllabus, with two changes. They are listed here so that every code in the plan has a page. They have no outlines in this draft.

  • Functional Bengali Language (GEBL-2401) moves from semester 4 to semester 8, to make room for Calculus II. This needs the university's approval (Decision 25).
  • URBS-4802 no longer exists and is removed (Decision 26).
